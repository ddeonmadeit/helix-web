WHAT WE BUILD

Our Offering

Affordable House & Land

New home and land packages across Adelaide suburbs — priced to be accessible for owner-occupiers, first-home buyers and investors.

7-Star Energy Rating

Every Co-Built home is designed to achieve a 7-Star NatHERS energy rating — that's 30% less heating and cooling cost than a standard 6-star home.

Sustainable Design

Solar-ready layouts, high-performance insulation, cross-ventilation and passive solar orientation built into every design from the start.

Turn-Key Delivery

Fully completed and move-in ready — no lock-up surprises, no hidden completion costs. What you see on the contract is what you get at handover.

Investment Properties

House and land packages suited to investors, SMSF buyers and FIRB-eligible purchasers. Strong rental yields across selected Adelaide growth corridors.

Property Concierge

End-to-end support from site selection and finance to build and handover — including access to off-market packages not listed on the major portals.
