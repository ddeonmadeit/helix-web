What we build

Three things,
done properly.

No flat-pack fitouts, no half-finished decks, no surprises mid-reno. Three service lines, the same standard across every one of them.

01

Decks

Hardwood and composite decking, pool surrounds, raised platforms, screening and pergolas. Built to handle a Queensland summer and the wet season after it.

  • Spotted gum, merbau, blackbutt
  • Pool-compliant balustrades
  • Screened privacy walls
02

Renovations

Whole-home, single-room, structural, cosmetic. We work in lived-in homes — site is left clean every day, rooms walled off, dust kept where it belongs.

  • Bathroom & kitchen rebuilds
  • Wall removals & openings
  • Extensions & second-storey adds
03

Internal fitouts

Kitchens, butler's pantries, built-in wardrobes, custom joinery and storage. Cabinetry that fits the wall, not a flat-pack catalogue.

  • Custom kitchens & pantries
  • Built-ins & walk-ins
  • Bathroom vanities & mirror cabinetry
How a job runs

Four steps.
No surprises.

  1. 01

    On-site quote

    We come out, look at the job, take measurements and photos. Free, and no obligation past the conversation.

  2. 02

    Written scope

    An itemised, plain-English scope and price. Fixed where we can fix it, hourly where we honestly can't.

  3. 03

    Build & tidy

    Job site stays clean, materials covered, off-cuts taken away. Your house is still your house while we're in it.

  4. 04

    Walk-back

    Final walk-through with you. Anything that's not right gets fixed before we send the invoice.
