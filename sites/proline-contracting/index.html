What we do

Cut. Drill.
Done.

  1. 01

    Concrete sawing

    Road, joint, flush, electric and demo sawing. Wet-cut where it counts, dust controlled where it doesn't. Lines stay straight.

  2. 02

    Core drilling & holes

    Diamond core holes through slabs, walls and footings — for plumbing, electrical, hydraulic, anchors. Sized to spec, plumb every time.

  3. 03

    Wall & floor chasing

    Chases for conduits, hydraulic and rebar exposure. Cut, removed and bagged out — leaves a clean run for the next trade.

  4. 04

    Demolition & cut-and-remove

    Sectional demo, hard removals, controlled break-outs. Concrete chunks loaded out, site swept down before we leave.

  5. 05

    Grinding, sealing & epoxy

    Floor grinding, polishing, sealing and decorative epoxy coatings. Slabs left flat, sealed and ready for traffic.

  6. 06

    Patching, pressure clean & test/tag

    Concrete rectification and patching, external pressure cleaning, and on-site safety test & tag. Site finished tidy, gear certified.
