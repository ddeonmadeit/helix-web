What We Make

Every cup,
chosen well.

  • 01

    Espresso & Milk Drinks

    Flat white, latte, cappuccino — the classics pulled from beans we roasted ourselves. Clean, balanced, no compromises.

  • 02

    Pour-Over

    Single-origin, brewed to order. One cup at a time, at the pace it deserves. The flavours show up when you slow down.

  • 03

    Batch Brew

    Filter coffee done properly — precision-brewed in small batches. Consistent, clear, something to come back for.

  • 04

    Chinese Teas

    A curated tea list alongside the coffee program — oolong, green, white and more. For when the moment calls for something different.

  • 05

    Take-Home Beans

    Roasted in-house by a certified Q grader. Traceable origins, honest roast profiles — take a bag and keep going at home.

  • 06

    Dine In

    A small, considered space on William Street — pull up a seat, take your time, and actually taste what's in the cup.
