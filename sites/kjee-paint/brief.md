## Research

## Voice
Direct, residential — Salisbury suburban painter, not industrial. Plain English, action-led, no agency-speak.

## Design
- Dark default + light toggle (CSS custom properties on `[data-theme]`, persisted in localStorage with pre-paint script).
- Palette pulled from the hero photo: vivid yellow accent on near-black surface (`#E8C200` on `#0E0D0B`). Light theme uses deep gold (`#8B7200`) on warm cream (`#FAFAF7`) for AA contrast.
- Anton condensed display + Inter body — confident, scannable, distinct from JFM's Bebas while sharing the vocabulary.
- Service list is a numbered row layout — image only where a real photo exists (interior, exterior, drywall). No placeholder watermarks needed because design doesn't require photos for the other rows.
- Mobile sticky bottom action bar, faceted clip-path CTAs, ticker strip between hero and services, JFM-style ghost numerals + border-grow hover accents.
