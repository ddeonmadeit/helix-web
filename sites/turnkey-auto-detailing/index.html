Services

From a quick refresh
to full paint correction.

Exterior Detail

Hand wash, decontamination, clay treatment, machine polish and seal. The everyday detail done properly — no swirls, no shortcuts.

Interior Detail

Vacuum, steam, shampoo and protect — seats, trim, headliner, vents. Stains lifted, leather conditioned, every surface left fresh.

Paint Correction

Multi-stage machine correction — swirls, holograms and light scratches removed. Glass-finish gloss before any coating goes down.

Ceramic Coating

Long-term protection for paint, wheels and trim. Hydrophobic finish, deep gloss, easier washing and UV resistance for years.

Headlight Restoration

Yellowed or hazy lenses sanded, polished and sealed. Better night vision, fresh-from-the-dealer clarity — without replacing the housings.

Engine Bay

Steam, degrease and dress — safely. Plastics restored, hoses revived, dust and grime cleared without soaking what shouldn't get wet.
