What We Do

Outdoor work,
kept simple.

    Lawn Mowing & Edging

    Regular cuts done at the right height for the season. Crisp edges along paths, beds and driveways — and every clipping picked up before we leave.

  • 02

    Garden Maintenance

    Weeding, pruning, deadheading and bed tidying. Ongoing care so your garden stays looking the way it did the day it was planted.

    Hedge & Tree Trimming

    Hedges shaped clean, small trees brought back into line, overgrowth lifted off fences and gutters. Tidy lines, mess hauled off.
