What we do

Plant. Crew.
Site sorted.

  1. 01

    Excavation & grading

    Bulk and detail excavation, cut-to-fill, batters and finished site grading. Tracked plant, surveyed levels, finished to spec.

  2. 02

    Demolition & concrete breaking

    Structure demolition, concrete breaking and hauling, driveway removal. Hard hats, dust control, tidy site every shift.

  3. 03

    Foundations

    Footings, slab prep, pad and strip foundations dug to engineer's drawings. Set out, dug, cleaned and ready for the concreter.

  4. 04

    Augering

    Post hole and large-diameter augering — fences, footings, signage, balustrades. Clean cores, no over-bore.

  5. 05

    Dirt & debris removal

    Spoil out, fill in. Tipper trucks on call for bulk dirt removal, debris and waste haulage. Tipped at licensed sites only.

  6. 06

    Lot clearing & site prep

    Vegetation, stumps, debris and old infrastructure cleared off. Site prepped flat and ready for the build.

  7. 07

    Landscaping

    Hard landscaping integration with the civil works — retaining, stone, paths, finished levels. Soft landscaping coordinated.
