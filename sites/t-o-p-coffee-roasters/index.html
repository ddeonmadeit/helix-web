    We roast our own beans on site — clean, balanced cups with the subtle fruit notes that come from roasting with care, not at scale.

    Espresso

    Rich, considered espresso drinks pulled from our own roast. The everyday cup, done properly.

  • 03

    Batch Brew

    Filter coffee by the cup — a lighter, cleaner way to taste what the beans are really doing.

  • 04

    Milk Alternatives

    A full range of alternative milks, so every preference and dietary need is covered without a second thought.

    A hand-picked selection of Chinese teas alongside the coffee — for the days you want something quieter.
