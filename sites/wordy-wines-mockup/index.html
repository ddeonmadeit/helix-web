About Wordy Wines

We started Wordy Wines because we were sick of turning up to birthdays, housewarming parties and awkward office Christmas functions with a bottle that said absolutely nothing. Wine should come with a label that means something. Or at least says something that makes everyone laugh.

Every label is designed in Australia. Every bottle is $25. Every single one is a better gift than a candle.

We've got labels for birthdays, retirements, new homes, new babies, weddings, divorces, teachers, nurses, besties, dads, mums — and everything in between that doesn't quite have a category yet.

Australia-wide delivery. Free shipping on orders over $100. 20% off any 6 bottles — mix and match across every collection.
