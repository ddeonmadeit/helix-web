What We Stock

Small range,
chosen well.

  • 01

    Food & Nutrition

    Dry, wet and raw — brands we'd feed our own. Tell us age, size and fussy or not, and we'll point you to the right shelf.

  • 02

    Treats & Chews

    Training treats, long-lasting chews, single-ingredient jerky. The good stuff — no rainbow-coloured mystery shapes.

  • 03

    Toys & Enrichment

    Puzzle feeders, snuffle mats, durable tug and fetch. Picked for chewers, thinkers and the easily-bored.

  • 04

    Beds & Comfort

    Cozy crates, orthopaedic mats, cooling beds for the warmer months. Sized properly, washable where it counts.

  • 05

    Collars, Leads & Accessories

    Everyday harnesses, training leads, ID tags, travel bowls and bandanas. Comfortable, sturdy, fairly priced.

  • 06

    Grooming & Small Pets

    Shampoos, brushes and nail tools for cats and dogs — plus food and supplies for birds, rabbits and small animals.
